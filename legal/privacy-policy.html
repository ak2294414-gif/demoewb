Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2025 · Compliant with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023 (DPDPA)

1. Who We Are

Vastralaya ("we", "us", "our") is a fictional online clothing store operated for demonstration purposes by NorthWeb Co. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle personal data when you use our website.

2. Data We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to operate our service:

  • Contact details: Name, email, phone number — to process and deliver your order.
  • Delivery address: Required to ship your purchase.
  • Payment information: Processed securely by our payment gateway. We never store card details.
  • Essential cookies: Session and preference cookies only. No tracking cookies, no advertising cookies.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To fulfill and deliver your order
  • To send order confirmation and shipping updates
  • To handle returns and customer support queries
  • To comply with legal obligations under Indian law

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not use it for profiling.

4. Your Rights (DPDPA 2023)

Under India's DPDPA 2023, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Erase your data (right to be forgotten)
  • Withdraw consent at any time
  • Nominate a representative to exercise your rights

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@vastralaya.in.

5. Data Retention

We retain order data for 7 years as required by Indian tax law (GST compliance). After this period, personal data is deleted.

6. Security

We use industry-standard SSL/TLS encryption, access controls, and regular security audits to protect your data.

7. Cookies

We use only essential cookies for site functionality (session management, cart, cookie consent preference). See our Cookie Policy for details.
