Cookie Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2025

What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device. We use only essential cookies — no tracking, no advertising cookies.

Cookies We Use

  • nx_cookie_v2 — Remembers your cookie consent choice. Expires in 365 days.
  • nx_cart_v1 — Stores your shopping cart items in your browser. Session-based, stored in localStorage.
  • nx_wish_v1 — Remembers your wishlist items locally. Not sent to our servers.

We use no third-party analytics, no Google Analytics, no Facebook Pixel, no advertising cookies.

Your Choices

You can decline non-essential cookies using the banner when you first visit the site. Essential cookies cannot be disabled without breaking site functionality (your cart would not work).

Managing Cookies

You can clear all cookies through your browser settings at any time. This will reset your cart and preferences.

DPDPA Compliance

Our cookie use complies with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023. We obtain consent before setting any non-essential cookies.
