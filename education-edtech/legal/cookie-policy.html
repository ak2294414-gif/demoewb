Last Updated: 2026-10-31

This Cookie Policy explains how Education & EdTech ("we", "us", or "our") uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website. This policy complies with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA) of India and applicable IT Rules.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They help the website remember your preferences and improve your experience. Cookies cannot execute code or deliver viruses.

2. Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies (no consent required — necessary for the site to function):

  • cookie_consent — Stores your cookie consent preference. Expires: 1 year. Provider: LearnHub (1st party).
  • Session cookies — Temporary cookies required for form submission and navigation. Expire: when browser is closed.

Analytics Cookies (only with your consent):

  • _ga, _gid — Google Analytics for anonymised traffic statistics. Expire: up to 2 years. Provider: Google Ireland Ltd.

We do not use advertising, tracking, or profiling cookies.

3. Your Consent

When you first visit our Site, you will see a cookie consent banner. You may accept or decline non-essential cookies. You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser cookies and revisiting the Site. Essential cookies cannot be disabled as they are necessary for the Site to function.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings:

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Cookies
  • Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Disabling cookies may affect website functionality.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Where we embed third-party content (e.g., Google Maps, YouTube videos), those providers may set their own cookies subject to their own privacy policies. We do not control third-party cookies.

6. Data Retention

Cookie data linked to your personal data is retained only as long as necessary per our Privacy Policy. Analytics data is anonymised after 26 months.
