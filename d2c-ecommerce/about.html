Our Journey

From a small idea to your wardrobe

Vastralaya started in 2020 when our founder, frustrated by the gap between Indian artisan quality and what was available online, decided to build a better bridge. The name comes from the Sanskrit for "house of clothing" — and that's exactly what we want to be.

Today we partner with weavers in Varanasi and Kanchipuram for our silk sarees, block-printers in Jaipur for cotton collections, and manufacturer clusters in Tiruppur and Surat for our contemporary styles.

Every piece tells a story of the hands that made it. We just make sure those stories find the right audience.
