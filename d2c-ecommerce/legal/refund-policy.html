Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2025

7-Day Return Window

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, with tags attached and in original packaging.

Eligible Items

  • All clothing in original, unused condition
  • Items with manufacturing defects (reported within 48 hours of receipt)
  • Wrong items delivered

Non-Eligible Items

  • Items worn, washed, or altered
  • Intimates and inner wear (hygiene)
  • Customised or monogrammed items
  • Items on final sale / clearance

How to Return

1. Email returns@vastralaya.in with your order number and reason.
2. We will send a prepaid return label within 24 hours.
3. Pack items securely and drop off at the nearest courier point.
4. Refund is processed within 5–7 business days after we receive the item.

Refund Method

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. UPI/Card refunds take 3–5 business days. COD refunds are processed via bank transfer within 7 business days.

Damaged or Wrong Items

If you receive a damaged or incorrect item, photograph it and email returns@vastralaya.in within 48 hours. We will arrange an immediate replacement or full refund.

Exchange Policy

We offer size exchanges once per order. Email us within 7 days to request a size exchange. Subject to stock availability.
