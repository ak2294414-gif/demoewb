Sarees

All sarees are free size — standard Indian saree length of 5.5 metres with an unstitched blouse piece of 80 cm. Suitable for all sizes. Blouse piece can be tailored to your measurements.

Saree draping tutorials are available on our Instagram. Contact us if you need help choosing the right saree for your body type.

How to Measure

  • Chest/Bust: Measure around the fullest part of your chest, keeping the tape parallel to the floor.
  • Waist: Measure around the narrowest part of your natural waist.
  • Hip: Measure around the fullest part of your hips, approximately 20 cm below the waist.
  • Shoulder: Measure across the back from one shoulder seam to the other.

If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up for comfort. For more help, contact us or WhatsApp us with your measurements.
