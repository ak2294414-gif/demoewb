Our Story

Fashion rooted in Indian craft, made for modern living

Vastralaya was born from a belief that Indian textiles deserve a global stage. We work directly with artisans and weavers across India — from Varanasi's silk weavers to Rajasthan's block-printers — to bring you authentic pieces at honest prices.

Every kurta, saree, and tee is quality-checked before it reaches you. We believe fashion should feel as good as it looks.
